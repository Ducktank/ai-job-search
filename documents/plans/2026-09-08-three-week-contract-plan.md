# Three-Week Contract Revenue Plan

**Window:** Sept 8 to Sept 28, 2026
**Goal:** billable contract work landed and invoicing before the window closes
**Written:** Sept 8, 2026. **Last reviewed:** Sept 9, 2026 (day 2 of 21)

The premise of this plan is that platform applications are slow and warm contacts are fast. Everything below is ordered around that. Toptal and Upwork are worth starting on day one because their pipelines take one to three weeks to produce anything, not because they are the likely source of the first check.

## Week 1 (Sept 8 to 14): build the pipeline

- [ ] Bid on five or more Upwork jobs per day. Filter for model optimization, inference tuning, Snowflake and Power BI work, and data pipeline builds. Five a day is thirty-five over the week, which is the volume that makes a low reply rate survivable.
- [ ] Work the former client and colleague list, every single name. This is the fastest path to money in three weeks, faster than any platform, and it is the item most likely to get skipped because it feels like asking for a favor. It is not. It is telling people who already know the work that capacity is available now.
- [ ] Complete the Toptal technical screening as soon as it is scheduled. Do not let it slip a week for prep. The screen tests what is already known.
- [ ] Call the IRS about LLC entity classification, including Form 8832 if electing corporate treatment. The form and its instructions are at [irs.gov Form 8832](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-8832). Decide on adding a spouse to the LLC before filing, because the answer changes the filing. An hour with a CPA is worth paying for here rather than working it out from the instructions.

**Acceptance for Week 1:** thirty-five or more bids out, every warm contact reached once, Toptal screening either completed or on the calendar, entity question decided.

### Daily cadence for the bidding weeks

Roughly two hours, front-loaded in the morning while the job board is fresh. Bids placed within an hour of a posting going up get read; bids placed on day-old postings mostly do not.

1. Scan new Upwork postings and place the day's five bids.
2. Two warm-contact messages, one follow-up on an earlier one.
3. Check and answer everything in the inbox from platforms and contacts.
4. Log what went out in the tracking table below.

## Rough revenue math

Useful mainly as a sanity check on what counts as enough.

| Scenario | Rate | Hours per week | Weekly |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conservative, part-time | $85 | 20 | $1,700 |
| Mid, part-time | $110 | 20 | $2,200 |
| Mid, near full-time | $110 | 35 | $3,850 |
| Top of range, part-time | $150 | 20 | $3,000 |

Two part-time engagements at the middle of the range clear more than one full-time one at the bottom, and they carry less risk if a client goes quiet.
